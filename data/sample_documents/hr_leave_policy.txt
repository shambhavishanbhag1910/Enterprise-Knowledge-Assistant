HR Leave Policy

Employees are eligible for 18 days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance through the HR portal.

Sick leave is allowed up to 10 days per year. For sick leave beyond 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate is required.

Maternity leave and paternity leave are provided as per company policy and local labor laws.

Employees must receive manager approval before taking planned leave.
